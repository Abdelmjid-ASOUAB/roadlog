Privacy Policy

Roadlog: Trip & Mileage Log

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Roadlog (“the app”) is a trip journal for iPhone made by Abdelmjid ASOUAB. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Your trips stay on your iPhone

  • Roadlog records your location while a trip is in progress, to draw its route and work out distance, time and speed.
  • Trips, routes, place names, notes, categories, stats and settings are stored only on your device. Roadlog has no account and no server, and does not upload your location or trips anywhere.
  • Place names are looked up with Apple's geocoding service, which receives the start and end coordinates of a trip. Apple's privacy policy applies to that service.
  • If you turn on automatic trips, Roadlog uses location in the background and your iPhone's motion data to notice when you start driving. This information is used on the device only.
  • You can delete any trip in the app, or delete everything by removing the app.

Exports

Mileage reports (PDF or CSV) are created on your device. They leave your device only if you choose to share them.

Advertising

Roadlog shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about the ads you see and interact with.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first, and can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iOS, the app asks permission to track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier.
  • Roadlog never shares your trips or GPS location with advertisers.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Roadlog is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
