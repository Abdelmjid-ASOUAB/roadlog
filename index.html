Roadlog

Trip & Mileage Log for iPhone

Roadlog keeps a journal of every drive: the route, start and end places, time and distance. You can name trips, mark them Personal, Business or Commute, and export a monthly mileage report as PDF or CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Trips aren't recorded automatically

Turn on Settings → Record trips automatically in Roadlog, then set location access to Always in the iPhone Settings app (Settings → Apps → Roadlog → Location). With “While Using the App”, trips are only detected while Roadlog is open.

The speed is different from my car's speedometer

Roadlog measures speed with GPS. Car speedometers usually read a little high, so a small difference is normal.

How do I export my mileage?

On the Trips tab, tap the Export button, pick a month and a category (for example Business), choose PDF or CSV, and share it.

How do I change the language?

Roadlog follows your iPhone's language. To use another language for Roadlog only: Settings → Apps → Roadlog → Language. Roadlog is available in English, Spanish, French and Arabic.

Where are my trips stored?

Only on your iPhone. Roadlog has no account and doesn't upload your location. Deleting the app deletes your trips.

How do I change my ad choices?

In Roadlog, go to Settings → Privacy choices (shown where consent applies). You can also change tracking permission in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Always follow traffic laws. Set Roadlog up before you drive, and don't use your phone while driving.
